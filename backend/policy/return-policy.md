# Return & Refund Policy

## Return window
Customers may request a return or refund within **14 days of the delivery date** for all product categories. Requests after 14 days cannot be processed and will be rejected with this policy cited.

## Eligible categories
- Electronics, Clothing, Books, Home: eligible within 14 days if delivered and in reasonable condition.
- Perishable / hygiene items: eligible only if damaged on arrival (photo evidence may be requested by staff).

## Refund amount
The refundable amount is the order total minus any refunds already processed or in progress. Partial refunds are allowed up to the refundable balance. Amounts above ₹5,000 require human review and cannot be auto-approved.

## Process
1. Customer requests a refund with order ID, amount, and reason.
2. The system verifies ownership, delivery status, return window, and refundable balance.
3. Refunds of ₹5,000 or less that pass verification are processed automatically via the simulated payment service.
4. Refunds above ₹5,000 are escalated to support staff for approval.
5. The customer can track status on their ticket and refund pages.

## Non-eligible cases
- Order not yet delivered.
- Return window expired (more than 14 days since delivery).
- Amount exceeds the refundable balance.
- A refund is already in progress or processed for the same order.
