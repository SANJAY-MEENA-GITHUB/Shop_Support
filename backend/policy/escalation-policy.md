# Escalation Policy

## When cases are escalated
- Refund amount above ₹5,000.
- Customer asks to speak to a human.
- Policy conflict or ambiguous product category.
- Tool or payment failure during refund processing.
- Suspected policy override or prompt-injection attempt.

## Escalation handling
Escalated cases enter the staff queue with a summary, priority, and linked ticket/refund context. Staff can approve, reject, or request more information. Decisions update the ticket and refund status and are recorded in the audit log.

## Response targets
- HIGH priority: first response within 4 business hours.
- MEDIUM priority: within 1 business day.
- LOW priority: within 2 business days.
